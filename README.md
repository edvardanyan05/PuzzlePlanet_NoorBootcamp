# PuzzlePlanet 🧩🌍

A relaxing and visually satisfying Android puzzle game where players reconstruct beautiful pixel-art images by swapping block groups into their correct positions.

Each puzzle starts with a complete pixel artwork. The image is then shuffled into large sections, and the player must swap them back into place before time runs out. As pieces reach their correct positions, they become locked, gradually revealing the completed artwork.

This project was proudly developed during the Noor Bootcamp by Noor Games.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Select

Tap and hold a puzzle piece to pick it up.

### Drag

Move the selected piece around the board.

### Swap

Drop the piece onto another piece to swap their positions.

### Complete

Place all pieces in their correct locations to restore the image.

### Hint System

If the player remains inactive for a short time, the game highlights two pieces that should be swapped.

### Win Condition

Place every puzzle piece in its correct position before the timer expires.

### Lose Condition

The timer reaches zero before the puzzle is completed.
